Plan Builder Board

Phase 2 screens for the multi-user app: first-run onboarding, the Plan tab where people add and change activities, and swapping an exercise on the day. Everything below is clickable. Example person: Ketan, home workouts with 2, 3, 5 and 10 kg dumbbells, plus treadmill and cycle.

1. Onboarding

Shown once, right after the first Google sign-in. One question per screen, a progress bar, back on the top left, and the button always in the same place at the bottom. Screens that don't apply are skipped, so a runner never sees equipment questions.

Question order and why

  1. Goal first. It sets sensible defaults for everything after (rep ranges, cardio amount), so later screens can pre-select an answer.
  2. Activities. Multi-select. Everything after branches on this.
  3. Routine or targets, per activity. Strength defaults to routine, cardio to targets. Targets say "coming soon" until Phase 3.
  4. Strength: equipment, then level, then schedule, then split. Split comes last because the valid splits depend on how many days you picked. Incompatible splits are disabled with a reason, and one is tagged "Recommended".
  5. Cardio setup for each cardio activity on a routine: days and current ability.
  6. Anything to avoid. Exercises you dislike (lunges) and joints to protect. The generator never picks those.
  7. Body stats are optional and skippable. They power the weight badges.
  8. Preview of week 1 before committing, so the plan feels concrete.

Try it: pick "Stay consistent", then Strength at home, Walk or run, and Cycle. Choose 4 days and watch the split screen.

2. Plan tab: change your activities any time

The Goals tab becomes Plan. Activities sit on top as cards; goals stay below. Adding running later, pausing cycling for a week, or moving from home to gym is one tap from here, and never touches days already done.

What to notice

  • Each card says how it works: Routine or Weekly target, which days, and the split.
  • Edit opens a full-screen editor with only that activity's questions, then shows exactly when the change starts: "Your plan changes from Mon, Oct 5. Days before that stay as they are."
  • Pause keeps the activity and its history but stops scheduling it. Useful for a week off the bike.
  • Add activity lists only what you don't have yet, and runs just those questions.
  • Profile gets a "Training preferences" row for goal, level and things to avoid.

3. Swap an exercise on the day

The checkbox still ticks. Tapping the exercise name opens swap options with the same movement, so a chest press stays a chest press. Options you can do with your equipment come first; the rest are shown dimmed with what they need.

What to notice

  • Same movement, ranked by your equipment. Dumbbell and bodyweight options first for Ketan. Barbell bench is shown but marked "Needs barbell, bench".
  • Just this day or every time. "Every time" is remembered, so future plans use your pick automatically.
  • Nothing is lost. A swapped exercise keeps its tick and XP, and shows a small "Swapped" tag.

4. Desktop

Same content, laid out for a wide screen: the Plan tab puts activity cards in a grid with goals beside them, and onboarding runs as a centered dialog instead of a full screen.
