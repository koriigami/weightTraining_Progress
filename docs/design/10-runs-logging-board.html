6. Every case, answered

A workout is in progress and you tap Log

"Finish or discard your workout first." One at a time, as today; the sheet shows Resume at the top.

You leave Log half done

The draft is kept, like a workout in progress. The bar reads Logging Push A · Continue.

You pick a time in the future

Not allowed, as Edit works now. Any past day is fine.

You log a day in a past week

That week is scored again: it can complete an old weekly goal or keep a streak going, and level up or badge moments still play.

The same routine is already logged that day

A gentle check: "You already logged Push A on Tue 29 Sep. Log another?" In case it was saved twice.

How long vs XP

XP and the daily bonus come from sets and cardio minutes, as always. How long sets the time shown on the workout, so you remember it right.

4. Consistency

Does training consistently pay today? Partly. Every training day (20 minutes or more) pays the +50 daily bonus, and meeting your weekly goal pays +50. So more training days a week already earn more. What earns nothing extra is keeping it up week after week: ten good weeks in a row pay the same as ten scattered ones.

A week withDaily bonusesWeekly goal (goal 3)Bonus XP
3 training days3 × +50+50+200
5 training days5 × +50+50+300
7 training days7 × +50+50+400

Three ways to reward keeping it up, tried on the same three people over 10 weeks. Rest days stay safe in all of them: they all reward meeting your own weekly goal, never training every day, and time away still never lowers XP.

Today and a count only the weekly goal bonus. b adds the streak milestones. c assumes a typical 400 XP week: because it adds to everything you log, it moves the most and makes every number change, which is its catch.

a. A growing weekly goal bonus My pick

+50 for meeting the goal, +10 more for every week in a row it is met, up to +100 from week 6. Miss a week and it starts from +50 again. Simple, visible on Home, and it only rewards your own goal.

🔥
Goal met 4 weeks in a rowThis week's goal pays +80 XP. +100 from week 6.

b. Streak milestones

+100 XP when the weekly streak reaches 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks. Easy to explain, but one training day a week keeps the streak, so it can't tell steady from barely.

c. A consistency score

The share of the last 8 weeks with the goal met, shown as a percentage, adding up to 25% to every workout's XP. The most precise, but hard to explain and it makes every number move.
