XP Rulebook

How Levl turns training into XP, levels, ranks and badges. These are rules v3 (October 2026): one daily bonus for 20 minutes of training, strength and cardio together, training days for the weekly goal and the streak, rest days and a comeback bonus.

New in v3Changed in v3Unchanged

Three ideas behind every rule

Effort, not loadA set earns the same 5 XP at 2 kg or 200 kg. How heavy you lift never scales XP.
Beat yourselfExtra XP comes from doing better than your own last time, or your own best ever. Nobody is compared with anybody else.
Show up, rest, come backThe daily bonus pays once a day when you train 20 minutes, lifting and cardio together. Rest days never cost you anything, and coming back after a week off pays extra.

Daily bonus, rest days and the streak

Strength training works best 2 to 4 days a week with rest in between, so nothing in Levl asks you to train every day. The streak is weekly, rest days are shown as rest, and a week off is answered with a comeback bonus, not a penalty.

Beat last time and records

Your best set of an exercise in this workout is compared twice: with the last workout that had that exercise, then with your best ever. The first time you do an exercise there is nothing to beat, so it earns neither.

Kind of exerciseBeat last time (+10)Record (+25 instead)
Weight and repsRows, curls, pressesMore weight with the same reps or more, or more reps at the same weightHeaviest ever, or the most reps at your heaviest weight
Reps onlyPush ups, crunchesMore reps in your best setMost reps ever in one set
TimePlankA longer holdLongest hold ever
CardioRun, walk, ride, treadmillMore km, or the same km or more in less time. Without distance: more minutes.Longest distance ever

Last time10 kg × 12
Best ever10 kg × 12

ResultRecord +25

Heavier than ever, so it's a record. Records win over beat last time, so this pays 25, not 35.

Levels and ranks

Level n starts at 50 × n × (n − 1) XP, so each level costs 100 more XP than the one before. There is no top level. The weeks column assumes the 645 XP steady week above, before any badge or goal XP, which makes it quicker in practice.

Pace stays as it is for now. After about 3 weeks of your testers' data, the Insights page shows how fast people really climb, and we look at the curve again.

Goals

Goals count your training: days that reached 20 minutes, sets, minutes and distance. Only progress logged after you create a goal counts, and once a goal is achieved it stays achieved. The reward is rounded to the nearest 5 and kept between 25 and 1,000.

When XP can go down

XP is worked out from your data, so removing data removes its XP. A level or rank can drop. There is never a demotion animation.

What happenedHow it shows
You delete a workoutThe confirm names the XP, and adds "You'll go back to level 6" only when a level or rank drops. After it, a toast: XP updated · Level 6.
You edit a workoutFewer sets, a lower weight, a removed exerciseThe same toast after Save, only when the level changes.
You move a workout's dateRecords and beat last time follow the order of datesThe same toast.
You change your weekly goalPast weeks are checked againThe weekly goal editor says so before you save. The same toast after.
You delete a weigh-inThe same toast.
Badge tiers are lostOnly through the deletes aboveThe badge shows as not earned again, with no moment.
v3 goes liveOne note on Home the first time: "XP was worked out again with the new daily bonus."
Goals no longer drop: once achieved, a goal keeps its XP and badge. Reaching a level again after a drop doesn't replay its level up moment.

When v3 goes live

  • Every workout is scored again with the v3 rules, so levels can move a little. Days that lost the finish bonus to an unfinished plan earn the daily bonus; days with two finish bonuses keep one. Level up moments you already saw don't play again.
  • Nothing you logged changes: only how it is scored. Plans are still kept, for the Clean Sweep badge.
  • Guards stay in place: workouts dated after tomorrow don't count, the server works out all XP itself, and there are limits on sets per exercise and exercises per workout.
