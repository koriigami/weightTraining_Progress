Round 2, signed off: option C, the mini hunter card, with the rank letter on the shield and no "XP to level" line. Also the day tiles, fixed everywhere, and what happens when you are away. Round 1 is kept below for the record.

1. The header

One cream card, the same cream, white edge and gold bevel as the Today card. The shield shows your rank letter (E, D, C, B, A or S), "Level 4" is written next to it, and the bar below says 113 / 400: XP into the level over what the level takes. Streak and this week's training days sit stacked on the right. The whole card opens Rank. Desktop keeps its hunter card.

2. Day tiles, the same everywhere

The green training-day tiles had their bevel drawn below the tile, 3 px, so they looked taller than the rest and the bevel covered the bottom of the gold ring that marks today. Now the bevel sits inside the tile, every tile in a row is the same height, today gets a complete gold ring with a small gap, and rest days get a thin border. The red dashed line runs through the middle of each row to show the alignment. Buttons and chips keep their bevel below: there it says "press me". The Statistics chips already had a border, so they only change colour on rest days.

3. Time away

In Solo Leveling a missed daily quest sends you to the penalty zone. Levl does not do that, and that is already how it works today, so nothing changes in the app.

XP, level and rank never drop with time

XP comes only from what you log. The Streak Keeper badge counts your best streak and goals stay achieved, so a month away leaves your level and rank exactly where they were. They only drop if you delete or edit workouts, a weigh-in or your weekly goal.

What a week off costs

A Monday to Sunday week with no training day ends the streak, and that week's +50 weekly goal isn't earned. That is the whole cost.

Coming back pays

The first training day after a week off earns the +25 comeback bonus, and Home says so while you're away.

No "missed days"

Days off show as Rest, never "missed": strength training works best 2 to 4 days a week. Losing rank for being away shames people at the moment they might come back, so there is no decay and no penalty.
