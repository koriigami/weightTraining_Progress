2. The reward stage

Your rank decides your chest (decided this round): Wooden at E, Silver at D, Golden at C, Crystal at B, Obsidian at A and Prismatic at S. A workout that earns badges opens one chest, your rank's, and every badge comes out of it, the best one last. Crossing a rank opens the new rank's chest for the first time, with the rank's title and profile frame inside. Monthly, Royal and the Pillow open for monthly, special and secret badges. Board 13 puts these chests on the Rank Road.

Pick a chest, press Play, then tap the chest in the phone. Your chest and badge options from sections 3 and 4 play here too.

3D could not start in this browser, so the stage shows a flat version.

Chest

Badges inside

  1. Drop. Falls, lands with a squash: a heavy wood thud (louder now), dust and a shake. The chest's name shows and its music starts.
  2. Taps. 1, 2 or 3 by tier. Each tap: the latch, a shimmer one note higher, more light from the seam. 3-tap chests charge for 0.8 s.
  3. Burst. A white flash, the lid opens with the creak and a bloom, sparks in the tier colour. The open chest moves down and stays on screen.
  4. Swirl. Light spirals into the chest. The medal rises on a curve, spinning, pauses backlit, then flips to face you with a flash, a ring and rising bells.
  5. Card. Framed in the tier colour. Coins fly from +XP to the level bar, and its number counts up with the XP-lines ticks.
  6. Next. Tap: the badge flies into the tray at the top and the counter drops.
  7. The end. The tray grows into the summary: badges, the total counting up, then Continue. No heading.

The full finish: tap Finish workout. A short drum roll and rise, Victory with its music, crowns and XP lines, the app's 1.8 s hold, then the chest drops.

A rank up: after Victory the E shield breaks into pieces, the D shield rises through a pillar of light with its title, then the first Silver chest drops. The D-Rank title and frame come out first, then the day's badges.

With reduced motion each step shows its end state at once, with no shakes or flashes. Sounds follow their own setting.

5. The 34 interactions

Each one is live: press, tap or play it. The pulse at the top right of a card shows where a haptic lands. Each card names its sound, which you can change in section 6. Changed marks what is new in round 2.

6. Sounds

Every slot, where you hear it, and its candidates. Tap a chip to hear it and use it; every demo on this board then plays your pick. Recorded sounds are CC0 (public domain) from Kenney's packs and OpenGameArt, with sources in docs/design/12-motion/sounds/LICENSES.md.

Checked, not guessed. I still cannot listen, so each candidate was analysed: its pitch over time, its key, and its loudness. Good moments only keep candidates that rise and sit in a major key; the tag on a chip says what the analysis found. Sounds made in code play in the key of the chest music.

7. Music

For reward moments only. The chest music loops under the opening, and its key sets the key of every reward sound. Level up and rank up are 4 s or less.

Music
50%. Reward moments only. Dips under big hits.
  • Plays alongside your own music. Levl sets the browser's audio session to "ambient", so Spotify or Apple Music keep playing under our sounds (Safari 16.4 and later; other browsers already mix).
  • Nothing plays during training except the quiet tap and set sounds. Music only in reward moments.
  • Sounds off means silence everywhere. Music has its own switch.
  • Reduced motion shows end states: no shakes, flashes, spins or confetti. Sounds still play.
  • Loudness: every file is levelled by its loudest moment (EBU R128) and limited, then each slot has its own volume: taps quietest, small successes a step up, rewards loudest.
