Board 11 · Round 1

Levl motion language

How Levl looks and moves in reels and posts, with two finished reels and two finished posts to judge it by. Every piece is rebuilt from the app as it is today, scored by the real XP rules, and scored with the app's own sounds. No stock footage, no AI voice.

The idea in one line

The videos should feel like the app's reward moments, not like ads about the app. So they are made from the same parts: the stroked Lilita type, the sky, the gold XP bar, the rank shields, and the slam, roll and sparks that already play on a level up. When someone opens Levl after seeing a reel, it should look and sound the same.

What makes it Levl

  • Real app pieces, rebuilt as vectors so they stay sharp: the Home header card, the week strip, the XP earned sheet, the shields.
  • Real numbers from the rules: 5 XP a set, +50 daily bonus at 20 minutes, +25 comeback, level 12 needs 1,200 XP.
  • The app's synthesized sounds (tick, thud, level up chime, sparkle) on a 120 BPM pulse, so every cut lands on a beat.

What we avoid

  • Slides, crossfades and zoom-ins on screenshots. Scenes change behind a bank of clouds instead.
  • Generated voices and stock music. Add a trending track in Instagram at low volume if you like; the reel works muted too.
  • The dark anime "System" look the clone apps share.

Sound

The reels use the sounds that are already in the app (lib/feedback.ts), rendered offline from the same code: the set tick, the level up chime, the rank up fanfare, the whoosh, the chest sparkle. Four new ones are made from the same parts: a thud for slams, a pop for chips, a hit for the boss and a crack for a broken streak. A soft kick and hat at 120 BPM sit under them so every cut lands on a beat. Over time these sounds become recognisable as Levl.

What could move into the app

Making these reels showed a few moments the app could play itself. Proposals only; each would get its own board.

  1. Chip to bar on Victory. Victory already rolls the total and grows the XP bar in one go. With chip to bar, each part of the XP (sets, daily bonus, weekly bonus) pops as its own chip and flies into the bar, so people see where their XP came from. My pick: yes, first.
  2. A welcome back moment. When the +25 comeback bonus lands, a small moment says "Welcome back" with the chip. It turns the app's kindest rule into something people feel. My pick: yes.
  3. Boss bar for a routine in progress. The workout screen shows the routine's sets as an HP bar that drains as you tick sets. Fun, but it changes a working screen. My pick: later, after testers ask for more game.
