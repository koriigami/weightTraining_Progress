How Levl explains itself

Two new things, built once and used for every release after: a short guide for someone who has just joined, and a What's new card for everyone else when something changes. Part 1 is the base for both: every screen of the app as it is today, taken from the live build with demo data and marked Stable or Changed in v11. Nothing in the app changes until you pick.

1. The app today How XP works 2. First-run guide 3. What's new 4. How it works 5. What I need from you

What changes and why

  • Today a new person lands on Home with no explanation. The four setup questions ask about units and equipment, never about XP, ranks or where things are. XP is explained only in pieces: the XP popover, the Victory lines, the XP earned card, one line in Settings.
  • Changes reach people only as the Home rules note ("XP was worked out again"), and a dated New pill in one menu. Nothing says what is new or how to use it.
  • So: a guide once, at the start, and a What's new card after each release that changes something people see. Both use the app's own game modal, so they look like Levl, not like a popup.
  • Only today's screens. The early boards are left out on purpose. The guide points only at things that are settled (the tabs, the Workout button, Home's cards), so it rarely needs rewriting. What changes later is told by What's new.

How XP works, in plain words

The rules today (v4, docs/design/xp-reference.html). The guide's last card and every What's new page use these words.

What you doXPThe fine print
Tick a strength set+5With at least one rep, or a 5 second hold. Weight and reps don't change it.
Cardio+1 a minuteUp to 30 a set, and +10 when you log the distance. Laps don't change XP.
Beat last time+10Once per exercise in a workout.
A record+25Instead of the +10. Your first time on an exercise earns neither.
Daily bonus+50Once a day, when the day's training reaches 20 minutes. That makes it a training day.
Weekly goal+50 to +100When the week's training days reach your goal. +10 more for each week in a row, up to +100. A missed week starts again at +50.
Comeback+25Your first training day after a whole week off.
Log your weight+10Once a day.
Badges and goals+25 to +1000Badges by tier, Bronze to Legend. Goals by how big they are.
Levels and ranksE to SE is levels 1 to 4, D 5 to 9, C 10 to 14, B 15 to 19, A 20 to 29, S from 30. Each level takes 100 XP more than the last. Time away never lowers your XP.

2. The first-run guide

It plays once, right after the four setup questions, on Home. About a minute. Skip ends it at any step, and Settings can play it again.

A. Spotlight tour My pick

A welcome card, then seven steps that dim the screen and light up the real thing: the level bar, Today, the Workout button, the week, then the Routines, Rank and Profile tabs. It ends with How XP works and one button, Start training. Why: it shows where things are on the real screen, works the same on a computer, and is done in under a minute.

Try it: tap Show me around, then Next.

B. Story cards

Four full-screen cards before Home, in the teaser stories' look: the game, the ranks, runs, logging later. Why not: it tells, but never shows where anything is, and people swipe through cards without reading.

C. First-visit tips

Nothing up front. The first time each tab opens, one small bubble explains it, with Got it. Why not: the explanation is spread over days, and someone who never opens Rank never learns about ranks.

4. How it works

When something shows

Open Levl→Just signed up?yes →Setup questions→Guide→Home
Just signed up?no →An update you haven't seen?yes →What's new→Home
An update you haven't seen?no →Home
  • One thing per open. The guide first, What's new next time. Never two cards in a row.
  • Only on Home, after any level-up or badge moments. Never during a workout, on Victory or on a shared link.
  • New people never see old updates. They start with every update marked seen, and get the guide instead.
  • People already on Levl get the October update once. The guide is waiting for them in Settings.
  • Several unseen updates show as one card, newest first, up to five pages.

What is remembered

Per person, on the server, so it holds on every device: guide done (yes or no) and last update seen (an id). Nothing on the phone, so the Privacy page needs no change.

Writing an update

One entry in a list in the code, with its pictures. A release without one shows nothing.

{
  id: '2026-10',
  date: '2026-10-02',
  pages: [
    { title: 'Laps for runners',
      text: 'Tap Lap as you run. Levl marks your fastest lap...',
      image: '/news/2026-10-laps.jpg' },
    { title: 'Your goal bonus grows', rules: true, ... },
  ],
}

The release steps get one more line: "Does this change something people see, or the XP rules? Add an update." I write it with the release and you read it before it goes live.

Seeing them again, in Settings

Two new rows in the App group. The row called Welcome tour today reopens the setup questions, so it gets the plainer name Setup questions.
