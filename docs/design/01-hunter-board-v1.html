Hunter Design Board

The visual reference for the workout app's game layer: rank shields, badge tiers, Strava-style monthly medals, the level-up and rank-up takeovers, and the Android interaction patterns. Tap anything that looks like it does something.

Ranks

Shield metal changes with rank. The letter uses the display face. S rank adds an outer glow.

Lifetime badges

Six tiers per family: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, then Master (obsidian with an ember rim) and Legend (prismatic, it shimmers). Shape tells you the family, metal tells you the tier. The next tier you haven't earned shows as a grey silhouette inside a progress ring.

Monthly medals

Earned once per calendar month, like Strava challenges. The ribbon carries the month. This month's unfinished challenge shows progress and days left.

Milestones

One-time specials. Star crest, always gold.

On the phone

Tick exercises, switch tabs, open the goal sheet. The page underneath never moves: sheets and snackbars float over it.

Today

What to notice

  • Per-exercise ticks. The circle fills, the check draws, and a gold "+15 XP" chip rises. Ticking the last strength exercise stamps the day as cleared. Cardio and core never block the streak.
  • Untick with Undo. Tap a ticked row. A snackbar appears above the bottom bar with Undo.
  • Bottom navigation. Four destinations, icon plus label, with the pill indicator behind the active icon. No burger, no content pushed down.
  • Rank chip. Top right: shield, level, and a ring showing XP toward the next level. Tapping it opens Profile.
  • Goal sheet. Step one shows only the six goal types. Continue opens that type's details. The sheet keeps one fixed height and the button stays pinned at the bottom on every step. Streak has no date field; the end date is worked out from your plan and skips rest days. Weight asks Lose or Gain, then shows the target weight and a pace meter.
  • Back button. In the app, Android back closes the sheet or celebration instead of leaving the page. Here, press Escape.

Example data: level 6, 108.8 kg latest weight, Sat Oct 10 as today. The app uses your real numbers.
