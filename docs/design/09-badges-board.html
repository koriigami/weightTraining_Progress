Badges, chests and the unlock moment

Round 1. Three fixes and a bigger idea. Tapping a badge you already have should show it, not replay "NEW BADGE!". Locked badges should never look like bronze. Every tier should get its own chest, Clash Royale style. Then a catalogue of new badges, serious ones and a few secret jokes, for you to pick a first batch from.

1. What's wrong now

Tapping an earned badge replays the unlock

The Badges tab calls the celebration again, so "NEW BADGE!" and the chest play every time you look. That was a v8 choice ("tap to watch it unlock again"); it feels like a bug. Fix: a view modal, section 2.

Every chest is the same brown

Only the medal and the ribbon change with the tier; the chest is always the wooden one. A Silver All-Rounder opens from the same chest as a Bronze Finisher. Fix: a chest per tier, section 4.

Bronze and locked look alike

A badge nobody has started shows its bronze medal, slightly faded, with a padlock. Earn it and you get the same medal, a little brighter. Fix: section 3.

The moment's line reads like a count

"Training days: 1 training day" is the Bronze threshold, not your total (you are at 3 of 10, on the way to Silver). Fix: "Bronze: your first training day", "Silver: 10 training days". And Milestones has only 2 badges: section 6 adds more.

2. Tap an earned badge: show it, don't replay it

The same game modal locked badges already use, with the colour, the tier and date, the ladder, how far to the next tier and the XP it has paid. A small "Watch the unlock" link keeps the replay for anyone who wants it. Unlocked rank gates on the Rank Road get the same kind of modal instead of replaying the rank up.

3. Locked vs earned: three ways

Earned cards in every option get their tier chip and a soft glow in the tier's colour. Only the locked treatment changes. Shown with Finisher (Bronze, 3 of 10 to Silver), All-Rounder (Silver, 8 of 9 to Gold), Road Runner (not started) and this month's Cardio Month (not yet).

Today

a. Stone My pick

Locked badges become grey stone with the icon as a silhouette. Colour means earned, always.

b. Ghost outline

An empty slot: a dashed outline of the shape and a faint icon. Clear, but quiet; a screen of them looks unfinished.

c. Dimmed next tier

The tier you are working towards, darkened, so you can see what is coming. Still colourful, so less clear than stone.

4. A chest for every tier

One chest shape, a material per tier, so the chest tells you what is inside before it opens. Monthly badges open from a chest in their own colour, the specials from a royal one, and the secret rest badges from a pillow.

5. The unlock moment: four ways

Each shows four frames and a Play button. With reduced motion, the last frame shows at once. Pick a tier on option A to see the chest, the shakes and the light change: one shake for Bronze, up to three for Legend, with a rainbow burst.

Tier up, for whichever option wins

Going from Bronze to Silver shows the old medal giving way to the new one, like rank up shows the old shield. The line names the tier and the target in words.

6. New badges

Everything here uses data Levl already stores: workouts with their finish times, sets, reps and weights, cardio kind, distance and time, holds, weigh-ins, goals, records, training days and comebacks. First batch marks my pick for the first release.

New badge families, Bronze to Legend

New monthly badges, +75 XP

Milestones, once each, +50 XP

Secret badges, 0 XP

Hidden as ? until you find one ("3 of 9 secrets found"). They pay no XP, so resting, odd hours or skipping legs are never worth farming. The jokes are always on our side, never at you.

XP check on the test history (31 workouts, 28 training days, level 12, C rank): the first batch adds +250 XP at the same tier values as today, or +175 at half. Level 12 stays 12. That history logs no weights, so Tonnage adds nothing there; a real lifter passes 5 t in a couple of workouts. The exact before and after for everyone is checked when we build it.
