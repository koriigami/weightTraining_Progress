Three new looks for the same app, built around your feedback. Pick one below, or use the switcher pinned at the bottom of the page. The whole board and both prototypes change with it. Tap "Play a moment" to see level-ups, rank-ups and badge unlocks.

Pick a look

All three share the same layout and flows. Logging screens stay calm in every look, so sets are easy to read mid-workout. The spectacle is saved for rewards.
