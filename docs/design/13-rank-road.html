6. Which chest opens Decided

Your rank decides your chest, the way each arena does in Clash Royale. After a workout that earns a badge, one chest drops after Victory, and it is your rank's chest; every badge comes out of it, the best one last. Crossing a rank opens the new rank's chest for the first time, with the rank's title and profile frame inside, and from then on your chests are that kind.

  • At most one chest per workout. Only a workout that earned a badge or crossed a rank drops one.
  • Crossing a rank: the title and the frame come out first, then that day's badges.
  • Monthly, Royal and Pillow open for monthly, special and secret rest badges. A rank crossing always wins.
  • Nothing new is stored. The chest comes from your level before and after the workout and the badges it earned, so it follows the rule that XP is worked out from your workouts, never kept as a total.

7. Should early levels be easier? Open

Not yet. The start is already quick with today's rules:

  • The slow part is the middle, not the start. The chests at each rank and the badges on the road give that stretch something to look at, without touching the level curve.
  • Changing the curve moves everyone at once. Levels are worked out from XP, so a cheaper curve would lift every current user's level the moment it ships.
  • So we measure first. The owner Insights page already shows the median days to D rank and the spread of ranks. In 4 to 6 weeks, if the median days to D is over 21, we make levels 2 to 5 cheaper in a plan of its own.

8. Later unlocks Open

Small unlocks every few levels would give the long stretch between ranks more to aim for, for example share-card backgrounds, frame colours or a pinned badge on your profile. Each needs art and a design of its own, so it would be its own board if you want it.
